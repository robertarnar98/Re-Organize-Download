# Re-Organize

Plan your Instagram grid on your Mac — drag, crop and arrange photos before you post. Nothing is ever posted or changed on Instagram.

## Download

**[⬇︎ Download the newest version](https://github.com/robertarnar98/Re-Organize-Download/releases/latest)** — click **Re-Organize.dmg** under *Assets*.

## Install (one time)

1. Double-click **Re-Organize.dmg**.
2. Drag **Re-Organize** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open it from Applications. That's it: Re-Organize is signed and approved by Apple, so it opens with no security warning.

## Updates

You don't need to come back here. When there's a new version, a blue **Update** button appears at the top of the app — click it and it updates itself in a few seconds. Your grids and photos are kept.

Needs macOS 15 Sequoia or newer · Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
