# Re-Organize

Plan your Instagram grid on your Mac — drag, crop and arrange photos before you post. Bring in what you've already posted by pasting your profile link. Nothing is ever posted or changed on Instagram.

## Download

**[⬇︎ Download the newest version](https://github.com/robertarnar98/Re-Organize-Download/releases/latest)** — click **Re-Organize.dmg** under *Assets*.

## Install (one time)

1. Double-click **Re-Organize.dmg**.
2. Drag **Re-Organize** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open it from Applications. That's it: Re-Organize is signed and approved by Apple, so it opens with no security warning.

## Updates

You don't need to come back here. When there's a new version, a blue **Update** button appears at the top of the app — click it and it updates itself in a few seconds. Your grids and photos are kept.

## Privacy

Re-Organize collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. It goes online for two things only: to look for updates on this page (that check sends nothing about you, your Mac or your photos), and, when you press Sync, to open the public Instagram profile you linked. GitHub, which hosts this page and the downloads, sees those requests like any web visit ([GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)).

**Instagram:** Re-Organize never signs in to Instagram and never uses your Instagram account. A pasted profile link only opens that public profile page, like a browser, when you press Sync. Instagram's terms don't allow automated reading, so syncing may stop working one day.
